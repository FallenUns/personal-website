Hello! I'm Patrick, a data scientist who loves building projects, from apps and data solutions to creative tools, and I am always experimenting with new technologies. I am a recent graduate from RMIT in Melbourne.

### What I work on

- **iOS development**: Blipt and Reffy, the RMIT First Health iOS Hackathon with Apple, Northern Health and Bilue, and the Apple Foundation Program
- **Full-stack development**: ERP systems, web applications and this portfolio
- **Research**: LLM privacy violation detection and NLP
- **Data science**: interactive dashboards, data cleaning pipelines, regression and deep learning models

### Skills

- **Programming**: Python, Swift, JavaScript and TypeScript, React, R, SQL
- **Data science**: data visualisation, interactive dashboards, statistical computing, data wrangling, ETL, spatial data analysis
- **Machine learning**: scikit-learn, TensorFlow, LLM evaluation, natural language processing
- **Mobile**: SwiftUI, Xcode, MapKit, Core Data, SwiftData, React Native with Expo
- **Web**: React, Three.js, WebGL, Shiny for R
- **Collaboration**: team projects, hackathons, industry partnerships

### Achievements

- Special mention at the RMIT First Health iOS Hackathon for Cliniwatch, a response to Northern Health's early warning challenge

- Academic research on detecting privacy violations with LLMs
